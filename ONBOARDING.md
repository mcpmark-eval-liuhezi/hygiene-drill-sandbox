# Welcome, new contributor!

We are so glad you found your way here. This repository is a friendly, low-stakes space for practicing everyday repository hygiene — branching, pull requests, reviews, and cleanups — without any fear of breaking anything. Take a look around, open an issue to say hello, and don't hesitate to ask questions: the only bad question is the one you didn't ask. Every contribution, no matter how small, is welcome and appreciated.
